Data Classification Policy

Tyndex Lab classifies internal data into three tiers:

Public
Information intended for public consumption: course catalogs, published
blog content, marketing material.

Internal
Information intended for staff and enrolled students only: internal
procedures, non-sensitive operational documents, instructor guides.

Confidential
Sensitive information restricted to authorized personnel only: student
personal data, payment information, staff records, security incident
details, unreleased exam content.

Handling requirements
- Confidential data must never be shared outside Tyndex Lab systems
- Internal documents should not be forwarded to individuals outside the
  organization without approval from a department lead
- Any AI assistant or automated tool operating on Tyndex Lab data must only
  surface information appropriate to the requester's role and must not
  disclose Confidential-tier data to students or the general public
