Attendance Policy

Live sessions
Students are expected to attend at least 80% of live instructor-led sessions
per program. Recordings are made available within 24 hours for students who
miss a live session.

Lab deadlines
Hands-on lab assignments must be submitted within 7 days of being assigned
unless an extension is granted by the instructor.

Extended absence
Students anticipating an absence of more than 5 consecutive days should
notify their instructor and may request a pause in their cohort timeline
(available once per program, up to 14 days).

Failure to meet minimum attendance without an approved absence may result in
removal from the certification track for that cohort.
