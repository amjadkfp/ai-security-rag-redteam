Academic Integrity Policy

Tyndex Lab expects all students to complete labs, assignments, and exams
honestly and independently unless collaboration is explicitly permitted by
the instructor.

Prohibited conduct includes:
- Submitting another student's work as your own
- Sharing exam or lab solutions with other students or online
- Using unauthorized AI tools to generate exam answers
- Falsifying lab completion records

First violation: written warning and required resubmission.
Second violation: suspension from the current cohort for 2 weeks.
Third violation: permanent removal from the program without refund.

Students may appeal a finding through the academic integrity review board
by emailing integrity@tyndexlab.example within 10 business days.
