About Tyndex Lab

Tyndex Lab is an AI and cybersecurity education organization offering hands-on
training in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, cloud security, and
emerging technologies. We serve students, working professionals, instructors,
and mentors through online programs, instructor-led classes, and practical
labs.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between theoretical learning and real-world
technical skill by giving students direct experience with the tools and
scenarios they will encounter in the field.

Programs offered:
- Cybersecurity Fundamentals (8 weeks)
- Offensive Security & Penetration Testing (12 weeks)
- Cloud Security Engineering (10 weeks)
- AI & Machine Learning Security (10 weeks)
- SOC Analyst Career Track (16 weeks)

Tyndex Lab was founded with the goal of making advanced technical education
accessible through practical labs rather than lecture-only formats. All
programs include hands-on lab environments and instructor mentorship.
