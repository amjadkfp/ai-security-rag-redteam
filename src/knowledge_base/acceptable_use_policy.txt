Acceptable Use Policy - Lab Environments

Tyndex Lab provides sandboxed lab environments for hands-on cybersecurity and
AI training. The following rules apply to all lab usage:

Permitted use
- Completing assigned lab exercises within the provided sandbox
- Practicing techniques taught in the curriculum against lab-provided targets
- Documenting findings for coursework submissions

Prohibited use
- Attempting to access, scan, or attack any system outside the assigned lab
  sandbox, including Tyndex Lab's own production infrastructure
- Attempting to exfiltrate lab environment credentials or configuration for
  use outside the platform
- Using lab environments to attack third-party systems on the internet
- Installing or running unauthorized tools not required for the assigned
  exercise

Violations of this policy may result in immediate lab access suspension and
review under the Student Code of Conduct. Serious violations, including any
unauthorized access attempt against production systems, are reported to the
Security team and may result in permanent removal from the program.
