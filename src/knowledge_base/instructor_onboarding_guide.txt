Instructor Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Tyndex Lab. This guide covers what new instructors need to know
in their first two weeks.

Week 1
- Complete HR paperwork and receive your staff credentials
- Attend platform training for the student portal and lab environment
- Shadow an experienced instructor for one live session
- Review the curriculum guide for your assigned course track

Week 2
- Co-teach your first live session with a mentor instructor
- Set up your grading and feedback workflow in the instructor dashboard
- Complete the FERPA and student data privacy training module

Ongoing responsibilities
- Hold weekly office hours (minimum 2 hours)
- Grade lab submissions within 5 business days
- Escalate any student conduct concerns to the Program Director

Instructors are paid on a bi-weekly cycle. Timesheets are due by end of day
Friday for the preceding two-week period.
