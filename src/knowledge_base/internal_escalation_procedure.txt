Internal Escalation Procedure

Purpose
This document outlines how staff should escalate operational issues that
cannot be resolved at the instructor or support-tier level.

Escalation tiers

Tier 1 - Instructor level
Handles: grading disputes, minor scheduling conflicts, general student
questions. Resolve within 2 business days.

Tier 2 - Program Director
Handles: cohort-wide issues, instructor coverage gaps, policy exceptions.
Resolve within 3 business days.

Tier 3 - Operations Lead
Handles: platform outages, vendor issues, cross-department conflicts.
Resolve within 1 business day due to higher business impact.

How to escalate
1. Log the issue in the internal ticketing system under the appropriate tier
2. Tag the relevant on-call lead
3. Include a summary, impact assessment, and any steps already attempted
4. Follow up within the resolution window if no response is received

Escalations outside business hours for Tier 3 issues should also be sent to
the on-call phone line listed in the staff handbook.
