Expense Reimbursement Policy

Eligible expenses
- Approved conference attendance and training materials
- Software/tools required for teaching, pre-approved by department lead
- Travel expenses for approved in-person events

Submission process
1. Submit receipts through the staff portal within 30 days of the expense
2. Include a brief business justification
3. Manager approval required for expenses over $200

Reimbursement timeline
Approved expenses are reimbursed within the next bi-weekly pay cycle.

Non-reimbursable expenses
Personal subscriptions, non-approved software, and meals outside of
pre-approved travel are not eligible for reimbursement.
