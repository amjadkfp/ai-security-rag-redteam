Certification Exam Rules

Eligibility
Students must complete all required labs and maintain 80% attendance to sit
for the Tyndex Lab certification exam.

Exam format
- 60 multiple-choice questions
- 2 practical lab scenarios
- 3-hour time limit
- Proctored remotely via webcam

Retake policy
Students who do not pass may retake the exam once at no cost within 30 days.
Additional retakes are available for a $75 fee.

Prohibited during exam
- Use of external AI assistants or chatbots
- Communication with other students
- Use of unauthorized reference material outside the approved exam
  environment

Violations of exam integrity rules result in automatic exam failure and
review under the Academic Integrity Policy.
