Career and Placement Support

Tyndex Lab offers career support to students enrolled in career-track
programs (Offensive Security, AI/ML Security, SOC Analyst Career Track).

Services included
- Resume and LinkedIn profile review
- Mock technical interviews with industry mentors
- Portfolio project feedback
- Access to the Tyndex Lab employer partner network
- Job posting board updated weekly

Eligibility
Career support is available to students who have completed at least 75% of
their program and maintained good academic standing.

How to access
Book a session through the Career Services tab in the student portal.
Sessions are available Monday-Friday, typically within 5 business days of
booking.
