Password and Account Security Policy

Password requirements
- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a special character
- Cannot reuse any of the last 5 passwords
- Must be changed every 180 days

Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
MFA is mandatory for all student portal accounts, instructor accounts, and
lab environment access. Students must enroll an authenticator app during
onboarding.

Account lockout
Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts within 15 minutes. Locked
accounts can be unlocked via the self-service portal or by contacting IT
support after identity verification.

Shared accounts are strictly prohibited. Each student and staff member must
use their own individually issued credentials at all times.
