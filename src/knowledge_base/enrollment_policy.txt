Course Enrollment Policy

Eligibility
Students must be 18 years or older to enroll in any Tyndex Lab program.
No prior technical background is required for foundational courses. Advanced
tracks (Offensive Security, AI/ML Security) require completion of the
Cybersecurity Fundamentals course or equivalent experience, verified during
enrollment review.

Enrollment process
1. Submit an online application through the student portal.
2. Complete the placement questionnaire.
3. Attend a 15-minute enrollment call with an admissions advisor.
4. Receive confirmation and access credentials within 3 business days.

Deferrals
Students may defer enrollment to the next cohort up to 7 days before the
program start date, at no additional cost. Deferrals requested within 7 days
of start date are subject to a $50 administrative fee.

Cohort sizes
Cohorts are capped at 25 students to maintain instructor-to-student ratios
suitable for hands-on lab work.
