Security Incident Reporting Procedure

Any staff member, instructor, or student who suspects a security incident
(unauthorized access, data exposure, phishing attempt, suspicious account
activity) should report it immediately.

How to report
1. Email security@tyndexlab.example with subject line "SECURITY INCIDENT"
2. Include: what you observed, when it occurred, and any relevant screenshots
3. Do not attempt to investigate or remediate the issue yourself

Response times
- Critical incidents (active unauthorized access, data breach): acknowledged
  within 1 hour
- High severity: acknowledged within 4 hours
- Medium/low severity: acknowledged within 1 business day

All incident reports are logged and reviewed by the Security team. Reporters
are not penalized for good-faith reports, including reports of their own
mistakes (e.g., accidentally clicking a phishing link).
