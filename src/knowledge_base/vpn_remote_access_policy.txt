VPN and Remote Access Policy

Staff and instructors accessing internal Tyndex Lab systems remotely must
connect via the approved corporate VPN client. Split tunneling is disabled
by default.

Access requirements
- VPN access requires MFA in addition to standard credentials
- Sessions automatically time out after 45 minutes of inactivity
- Remote access logs are retained for 90 days for security auditing

Student lab environments are cloud-hosted and accessed directly through the
browser-based lab portal; VPN is not required for standard student lab
access.

Any staff member requiring VPN access from a new or unrecognized device must
submit a request to IT Support at least 1 business day in advance for
approval.
