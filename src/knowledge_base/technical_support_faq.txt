Technical Support FAQ

Q: I can't access my lab environment. What do I do?
A: First, check the platform status page. If labs show as operational, try
clearing your browser cache or using an incognito window. If the issue
persists, contact support@tyndexlab.example with your student ID.

Q: My certification exam won't load.
A: Ensure you're using a supported browser (Chrome or Firefox, latest
version) and that your webcam/microphone permissions are enabled for the
proctoring tool. Contact support at least 30 minutes before your scheduled
exam if issues persist.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. If you don't receive
a reset email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder, then contact
support.

Q: Can I access lab environments on mobile?
A: Lab environments require a desktop or laptop browser. Course videos and
portal browsing are mobile-friendly, but hands-on labs are not supported on
mobile devices.

Q: Who do I contact for billing questions?
A: Email billing@tyndexlab.example or use the Billing tab in the student
portal.
