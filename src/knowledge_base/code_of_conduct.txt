Student Code of Conduct

Tyndex Lab is committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and
professional learning environment. All students are expected to:

1. Treat instructors, mentors, and fellow students with respect.
2. Use lab environments only for their intended educational purpose.
3. Not attempt to access systems, accounts, or data outside the scope of
   assigned lab exercises.
4. Not share exam content, lab solutions, or certification materials outside
   their cohort.
5. Report any technical issues or safety concerns to their instructor or
   the support team promptly.

Violations
Violations of this code of conduct may result in a warning, temporary
suspension from lab access, or removal from the program without refund,
depending on severity. Serious violations (e.g., unauthorized access attempts
against Tyndex Lab infrastructure, harassment) result in immediate removal.

Appeals may be submitted in writing to conduct@tyndexlab.example within 10
days of a decision.
