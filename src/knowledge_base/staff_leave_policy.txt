Staff Leave Policy

Paid time off (PTO)
Full-time staff accrue 15 PTO days per year, accrued monthly. Part-time
instructors accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis based on contracted hours.

Sick leave
10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. Unused sick days do not carry
over to the next year.

Requesting leave
Submit leave requests through the staff portal at least 2 weeks in advance
for planned leave. Emergency/unplanned leave should be reported to your
manager as soon as possible, ideally before your shift or class begins.

Leave during active cohorts
Instructors with an active teaching assignment should coordinate coverage
with the Program Director before taking planned leave to ensure a
substitute instructor is arranged.
