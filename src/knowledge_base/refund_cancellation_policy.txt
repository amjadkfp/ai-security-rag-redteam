Refund and Cancellation Policy

Full refund window
Students may request a full refund within 5 calendar days of program start,
provided fewer than 2 lab sessions have been completed.

Partial refund window
Between day 6 and day 14 of the program, students are eligible for a 50%
refund of tuition paid.

No refund period
No refunds are issued after day 14 of the program, or after 3 or more lab
sessions have been completed, whichever comes first.

Cancellation by Tyndex Lab
If Tyndex Lab cancels a cohort due to insufficient enrollment, students
receive a full refund or the option to transfer to the next available cohort
at no charge.

How to request a refund
Submit a refund request through the student portal under Billing > Refund
Request, or email billing@tyndexlab.example. Refunds are processed within
10 business days.
